Stability Garage Doors LLC ("Stability Garage Doors," "we," "us") respects your privacy. Information we collect

When you request service or a quote, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code or service address, and details about your garage door issue and preferred timing. We use your information to contact you about your request, schedule and perform service, provide quotes, follow up on completed work, and improve our services and advertising. We may contact you by phone call, text message or email regarding your request.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with our own technicians and trusted service providers who help us run our business (for example, scheduling, communication and advertising platforms), and when required by law.

Advertising and cookies

We use tools such as the Meta Pixel and Google Ads to measure and improve our ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram and Google account settings.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide service and meet legal and business requirements, and we take reasonable measures to protect it.

Your choices
